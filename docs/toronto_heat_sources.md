# Toronto heat-warning criteria: verified sources (retrieved 2026-11-16)

| Fact | Source (date modified) |
|---|---|
| ECCC issues Heat Warnings; TPH stopped issuing its own in 2019. | toronto.ca, Additional Heat & Health Resources (2026-07-31) |
| Heat Warning (southern Ontario incl. Toronto): forecast of >= 2 consecutive days with Tmax >= 31 C and Tmin >= 20 C, or >= 2 consecutive days with humidex >= 40. | toronto.ca, About Toronto's Heat Relief Strategy (2026-04-30) |
| Heat Relief Network: 500+ cool spaces open all summer (May 15 - Sep 30), not only on warning days; Streets to Homes outreach does wellness checks for people living outdoors. | toronto.ca, Cool Spaces Near You; About Toronto's Heat Relief Strategy |
| 24-hour Cool Spaces exist (TSSS e-updates on opening/closing); Council strengthened the Heat Relief Strategy in Dec 2025 (item 2025.EX28.4). | toronto.ca, Heat Relief page (2026-07-19); Additional Heat & Health Resources |
| ECCC introduced colour-coded weather alerts. | toronto.ca, Additional Heat & Health Resources |
| Toronto heat-warning dates 2019-2026 (list below). | toronto.ca, Additional Heat & Health Resources |

NOT verified: the criteria for any tier above Heat Warning (e.g. "Extreme Heat Warning" /
"Extended Heat Warning"). The ECCC alert-criteria page (canada.ca) refused connections from the build
environment, and the 2025.EX28.4 council report (secure.toronto.ca) was not reachable. Check both before
quoting a second tier.
